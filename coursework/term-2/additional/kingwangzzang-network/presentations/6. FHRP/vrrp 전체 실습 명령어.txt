ip routing

! 1. [Tracking] Uplink 감시
track 1 interface FastEthernet0/0 line-protocol

! 2. Uplink 
interface FastEthernet0/0
 no switchport
 ip address 1.1.4.2 255.255.255.0
 no shutdown

! 3. Downlink (VLAN 30 할당) 
interface FastEthernet1/0
 switchport mode access
 switchport access vlan 30
 no shutdown

! 4. SVI & VRRP (VLAN 30 Master)
interface Vlan 30
 ip address 3.1.3.1 255.255.255.0
 no shutdown
 vrrp 30 ip 3.1.3.254
 vrrp 30 priority 150              ! Master
 vrrp 30 preempt delay minimum 60
 vrrp 30 track 1 decrement 60
 
! 5. Routing (EIGRP AS 100)
router eigrp 100
 no auto-summary
 network 1.0.0.0       
 network 3.0.0.0    
end
