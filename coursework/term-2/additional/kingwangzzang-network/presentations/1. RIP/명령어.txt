2. distribute-list

R1
conf t
router rip
version 2
network 10.32.0.0
network 10.64.0.0
no auto-summary
end

conf t
access-list 10 deny 10.32.0.0 0.15.255.255
access-list 10 permit any

conf t
router rip
distribute-list 10 out s1/0

R2
sh ip route

3. offset-list 설정

R1
1). ACL 만들기

conf t
